- [Stack a header above a body bitmap](./stack-header-and-body.md) — compose two pre-rendered bitmaps into one label.
- [Render text and an image into one label](./render-text-and-image.md) — combine `renderText` and `renderImage` output side-by-side or stacked.
- [Find the right gamma](./find-the-right-gamma.md) — empirical procedure for calibrating darkness per printer/tape combination.
